    How to dedupe a list online

    1. Copy your list (one entry per line) into the input panel.
    2. Choose how strict the match should be: ignore case, trim surrounding spaces, drop empty lines.
    3. Copy the clean list back into your spreadsheet, CMS or email tool.

    Works with any plain-text list

    Because the tool compares raw lines, it does not care what the list contains. Common cases: newsletter subscriber lists, product SKUs, tag lists, redirect URL lists, crawl dumps and exported analytics rows.

    Keep first or keep last?

    Keep first is safest for lists where the first occurrence carries the best data. Keep last is useful when you merge an older list into a newer one and want the latest copy to win.
