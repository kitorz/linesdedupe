    How to remove duplicate lines online

    1. Paste your text into the input box — one item per line.
    2. Pick your options: ignore case, trim spaces, remove blank lines, keep the first or last copy.
    3. Copy the deduplicated result or download it as a .txt file.

    Why use an online line deduper?

    Spreadsheets break on long text, messy CSVs and pasted logs. An online tool is faster: paste, read the live counter, and you are done. Because the work happens locally in JavaScript, private lists such as customer emails or internal logs never leave your device.
